From gap to empowerment

AI adoption stalls at the leadership layer, not the tooling layer.

Most organizations can buy AI tools. Far fewer have leaders who know how to redesign work around them, bring their people through the change, and make sound calls about what agents should and shouldn't do.

This assessment doesn't stop at diagnosing gaps. Every report maps results to concrete pathways to empowerment — for you, and for the teams you lead.

What it measures

26 parameters across five modules — plus an AI fluency check.

Executive Lite samples the framework in about 5 minutes. The full Self and 360 assessments cover all 26 parameters in about 30.

A7 PARAMETERS

AI Leadership Competencies

How you set direction, make decisions and model the behaviors an AI-enabled organization needs.

B6 PARAMETERS

Ability for Change

Your capacity to adapt, learn and lead others through fast-moving, ambiguous transformation.

C4 PARAMETERS

Change Readiness

How prepared you and your team are to adopt new ways of working right now.

D6 PARAMETERS

Organizational Enablers

The structures, resources and culture around you that speed AI adoption up — or hold it back.

E3 PARAMETERS

Psychological & Motivational Readiness

The mindset and motivation that determine whether change sticks.

KNOWLEDGE CHECK

AI Fluency

Scenario questions on how modern AI and agents really work — with the correct answers explained in your report.

FAQ

Questions leaders ask first.

Is it really free?

Yes — Executive Lite is free during the beta. After beta ends it will carry a fee.

How long does it take?

Executive Lite takes about 5 minutes. The full Self and 360 assessments take about 30 minutes. Your report arrives by email once our team has reviewed it.

Who sees my results?

By default, you're the only person in your company who sees your report. Access at AIISV is limited to a small group of designated reviewers.

Are the sample reports real people?

No. Every sample uses a fictional respondent, so you see the full structure and depth without anyone's personal data.

Can my whole leadership team take it?

Yes. The Senior Leader and People Leader Self and 360 reports are built for cohorts — email us to set one up.
